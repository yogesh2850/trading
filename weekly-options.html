How it works

A composite of trend, 12-week momentum, breadth (RSP/SPY), credit (HYG/TLT) plus the VIX term-structure stress rule sets the weekly posture. The playbook maps posture + IV to defined-risk option structures (debit spreads, credit spreads, iron condors) on liquid ETFs. Signals validated on 25 years of weekly data — positive in all five folds incl. 2008 & 2020, Sharpe 0.64 vs 0.52 buy-and-hold at half the drawdown.
